Questions wrap shops ask

Can I add the 3D configurator to the site I already have?

Yes. WrapMe drops into an existing site, so you can start with the configurator and leave the rest of your site as it is.

Which films can customers try?

Real catalogs from 3M, Inozetek, Avery and Oracal, the ones shops already quote from.

Can I keep my prices private?

Yes. Each service can show a price or say "inquire for pricing", and you can change it yourself at any time.

Do I need to know code to update it?

No. Services, gallery, team and FAQs are edited from a dashboard, and pages can be shown or hidden with a switch.
