What it is

Truck Guardian is a TerryCo company that has written vehicle service contracts and 24/7 roadside coverage for medium- and heavy-duty commercial trucks since 1961. Its site ran on Wix. I rebuilt it from the ground up as a fast static site and moved truckguardian.com onto it in September 2026.

The homepage on a computer and a phone. As you scroll, the truck drives off toward the mountains.

What I built

  • Nothing lost in the move. Every word on the old Wix site was recovered and catalogued before the rebuild, and the old Wix addresses forward to the new pages instead of breaking.
  • One page per program. Medium duty, heavy duty, certified used trucks and roadside, each with its terms, key limits and its own questions and answers.
  • A comparison table. All four programs side by side: what each suits, contract type, terms and deductibles.
  • Coverage you can see. A scroll-driven sequence turns the truck and stops at the engine, transmission, drive axle and emissions, lighting each part as its coverage comes up.
  • Built to be found. Titles and descriptions written for search, structured data, a social card, and a summary file for AI assistants.
  • Easy to keep up. Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no build step to host, generated from one template so every page shares the same nav and footer.
