TommyOS — Tommy Roldan, Miami Web Developer & Designer

TommyOS is the portfolio of Tommy Roldan presented as a desktop operating system in the browser: the Work folder holds websites, apps, e-commerce stores and AI agents; Games holds Lightwall, Brick Breaker, Meme Maker and Paint with Trace; Tools holds free browser utilities. On a phone it becomes a home screen. Every icon opens the real thing.
