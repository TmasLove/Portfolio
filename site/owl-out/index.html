Six games, and a different show every time

Every show opens under the owl and ends in the dark. In between come three of the four middle games, in an order nobody knows, each harder than the last.

The Watcher

Run while the owl looks away. Freeze when it turns. Anyone still moving is out.

Floor Drop

The floor falls away from the outside in. Find a tile and keep it

Hot Potato

Somebody has a bomb. Shove a friend to make it theirs before the fuse runs out

The Sweeper

Jump the low bar, slide under the high one, on a platform over lava. Both keep speeding up.

Colour Call

A colour is called. Every other tile drops into the sea. Less time each round.

Last Light

The final. Fewer lights than players, and each light saves exactly one of you. Shoving is encouraged.

Going out is half the fun

Get eliminated and you are carried up to the medical suite: a glass ward hanging over the arena where everyone still playing can see you. You get bandages to match what got you, a drip of something restorative, and two medics who are not impressed.

Then you watch the rest of the show from above.

Fall all the way down

Miss a tile and you do not vanish. You fall, loudly, to the bottom, and you bounce.

Same jumpsuit, your colours, your hat, your title

Everyone wears the suit. What you put on it is earned: surviving pays, winning pays more, and taking a rival out on purpose pays too. Spend it on hats, face and back gear, emotes and takedown effects. A few titles cannot be bought at all.

Get the test build

Free, and unfinished on purpose. Play a show against the bots, break things, then tell us what broke.

Windows

Zip file, about 60 MB Download for Windows
  1. Right-click the zip and choose Extract All
  2. Open the folder and run OwlOut.exe
  3. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway

Mac

Zip file, about 75 MB Download for Mac
  1. Double-click the zip to unpack Owl Out
  2. Right-click the app and choose Open, then Open again
  3. If macOS still refuses, go to System Settings, Privacy and Security and press Open Anyway
  • All six games are playable against bots. A full show takes about five minutes.
  • Playing with friends works on the same Wi-Fi: one hosts, the others type the host's address. Across the internet is not ready.
  • Both warnings above appear because the game is not yet signed with Microsoft or Apple. It is the same game either way.
  • Owl Out is a working title. The name, the price and the release date are not decided.
