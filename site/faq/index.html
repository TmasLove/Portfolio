Questions people ask.

Straight answers before a project starts.

Who builds websites for small businesses in Miami?

I'm Tommy Roldan, a web developer and designer based in Miami, Florida. I build websites, e-commerce stores, mobile apps, and AI agents for startups and small businesses, and I've been shipping production work since 2015.

What kind of projects do you take on?

Four kinds: fast marketing websites, e-commerce and Shopify storefronts, iOS and Android apps, and custom AI agents and workflow automation. If it lives on the web or in an app store, it is probably in scope.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope, because a marketing site, a Shopify storefront, and a custom web app are very different builds. Tell me what you have in mind through the contact form and you will get a straight answer on scope and cost before anything starts.

How long does it take to build a website?

Timeline follows scope and how quickly content and feedback come back. A focused marketing site moves faster than an e-commerce build or a custom application. Share your deadline when you reach out and I will tell you honestly whether it is realistic.

Do you build Shopify stores?

Yes. I build and customize Shopify storefronts, including custom theme work, product and checkout flows, and the brand design around them. Dolce Vita Supplements is a live Shopify build of mine.

Do you build mobile apps for iOS and Android?

Yes, both from a single codebase using React Native and Expo. Clear Care Dental is a full-stack dental benefits platform built this way, where members find in-network dentists, track plan usage, and book visits from one cross-platform app.

What is an AI agent, and do you build them?

An AI agent is software that carries out multi-step work on its own instead of only answering a question, such as researching, calling APIs, and updating your systems. Yes, I build custom agents and workflow automation, including a voice agent that handles live phone conversations.

Can you redesign or take over an existing website?

Yes, redesigns and rebuilds are a regular part of the work. NST Pharma and World Resort Rescue were both full redesigns of existing sites, rebuilt for structure, speed, and how they read on phones.

Do you work with Spanish-speaking clients?

Yes. I am fluent in English and Spanish, so the whole project can run in either language, and this site is published in both. For Miami businesses serving bilingual customers, I can build the site that way too.

Are you available for new projects?

Yes, I am currently taking on new projects. The fastest way to start is the contact form, where you can outline the project, the type of build, and your budget range.
