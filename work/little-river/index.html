What it is

Little River is my family office, a small building behind the blue tile mural on NE 2nd Ave in Little River, Miami. A few businesses are run from it, and the day-to-day work behind them (marketing, content, outreach, search and support) is done by a team of AI agents running on a server in the office. They draft, research and report; a person approves anything that goes out.

The command centre's Floor screen, an earlier version.

What the agents do

  • Content. Blog posts and social content for a dental group, written to its rules and checked against them before anyone sees a draft.
  • Outreach. Find businesses that fit, check them, and draft the first email, including a preview site for businesses with no website or a weak one.
  • Search and AI visibility. Watch Google and Bing for the sites in the estate, run audits three times a week, keep an eye on competitors, and turn what an audit finds into fixes.
  • Studio. Storyboards, still images and short commercials, rendered overnight while the office is empty.
  • Support. Draft replies to guest and customer messages for the team to send.

A person signs off

Nothing goes out on its own. Every email, post and reply arrives as a card that can be approved, denied, or sent back with notes, and the revision comes back for another look. The cards show up in the command centre and in a Discord channel, so approving work takes a tap from wherever I am.

Three ways in

The command centre

A desktop app for Mac and Windows, for me: an overview of the estate, every agent, search analytics, approvals, support, and every run. Locked with a password and Touch ID.

The staff console

A web console for the team. They work the support queue and hand jobs to agents in a small pixel town, one building per company, and the agent reports back.

The public site

littleriver.site: the building, a view-only version of the agent town, consulting, and a recording space that is on the way.
