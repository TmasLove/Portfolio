What it is

The Clear Care Dental Group member app, native SwiftUI, live on the App Store since September 30, 2026. Members see what they have saved this year and what their plan still covers, check local prices with and without the membership, review and accept the treatment plans their dentist sends, and carry their membership card for the front desk. They can also add a photo of a dental X-ray for a plain-language explanation and questions worth asking their dentist; the app is clear that this is an automated reading, not a diagnosis.
