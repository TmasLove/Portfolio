What it is

When someone steals your King of the Mountain, give it the send-off it deserves. Type in the segment, the day you took it, the day you lost it and who took it, and KOM Memorial drafts a funny obituary and draws a memorial card you can download or share. No Strava API, and no account.

What it does

  • Drafts an obituary from five templates; you can write another or edit it freely
  • Counts the days you held the crown
  • Draws the card in two sizes: a 1080×1080 post and a 1080×1920 story
  • Download the PNG, or share it straight from your phone
  • Runs entirely in your browser: no sign-up, no server, no uploads. Your draft is kept in this browser only
