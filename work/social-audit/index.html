What it is

Influencer analytics across six platforms — as a desktop app.

No server. No database. No hosting bill. Your API keys stay in your OS keychain and your data stays on your machine.

macOS · Windows · Electron + React

What it does

Find creators, audit them properly, and run the outreach — without leaving the app.

🔍 Discover
Find creators by what they post about, or by niche. Filter by country, language, audience size.
📊 Audit
Real engagement, estimated post pricing, sponsorship history, recurring topics, audience-authenticity signals.
✉️ Outreach
Business emails pulled from bios — and a button that reads a creator's link-in-bio page to find the one they didn't publish. Pipeline status per creator.
💰 Campaigns
Group creators against a budget. See what a roster costs before you commit.
📈 Track
Follower growth accumulated locally. Compare up to four creators side by side. Export to CSV.

The rule this codebase enforces

Every number says where it came from.
Measured
Computed from real data the platform's API returned.
Estimated
An industry formula applied to measured inputs. A model, not an observation.
Not available
The platform doesn't expose it. Nothing is shown rather than guessed.

A confident zero is worse than a labelled blank.
