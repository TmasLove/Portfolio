GRVT

Cycling streetwear · Miami · Founded 2015 · Laid to rest

It started with the bikes, not the clothing.

GRVT came out of riding fixed gear around Miami. The shirts were made for the people already out there — not for a market, and not for anyone who needed the reference explained.

It ran as a small label with a Shopify storefront behind it, then stopped. What follows is what it was.

A custom Shopify build that put the GRVT designs in front of anyone who wanted one, rather than just the people I rode with. It did the job it was built for.

Here lies GRVT.
The brand didn’t last. The designs still hold up.

No relaunch, no coming-soon, no mailing list. It ran, it stopped, and this is what it left behind — kept visible rather than quietly deleted, because early work you are willing to show is worth more than a portfolio with no failures in it.
